What We're Building

LucreShare is revenue-splitting software currently in development. Here is what we're aiming for.

Flexible Splitting

We're building a way to define how revenue is shared among the people who contribute to it.

Automated Calculation

The goal is to replace error-prone manual math with rules that are applied consistently.

Clear Visibility

We intend for everyone involved to be able to see how a split was worked out.

How We Plan It to Work

This is the intended flow. The product is still being built.

1

Define the split

Set who receives what share of revenue.

2

Apply it to revenue

LucreShare is intended to calculate each person's share automatically.

3

Keep everyone informed

We want every participant to be able to see how the revenue was divided.
